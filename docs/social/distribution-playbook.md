# Distribution Playbook — Growing From Zero

Last updated: 2026-07-01

The editorial calendar covers WHAT to publish. This file covers everything that happens OFF the account: getting the content in front of people who don't follow you yet. All guardrails apply here too: `voice.md` (tone, forbidden phrases), `crisis.md` (pause rules), no product in outreach — the free Wanted tool is the only thing ever offered.

Time budget assumption: ~30 min/day on top of content production. Sections are ordered by impact.

---

## 1. Shark Week Window (July 26 – August 1, 2026)

Discovery's Shark Week runs **Sun Jul 26 – Sat Aug 1, 2026** — the biggest free shark-attention window of the year, landing in relaunch week 4. The 2026 opener (K-Pop Shark Heroes, changing shark perception) is thematically identical to the brand's mission — perfect commentary material.

Plan for that week:

- **Use BOTH trend slots.** Trend slot A (Sun/Mon): Bureau acknowledgment Reel — one shot of Finnley in front of a tiny TV, on-screen text: `The Bureau acknowledges Shark Week.` / `Attendance has been declared mandatory.` / `Luna has requested the schedule in writing.` Caption first line contains "Shark Week". Trend slot B (mid-week): TikTok stitch/duet of a Shark Week clip with a Myth Correction Office correction (fact verbatim from `sources.md`).
- **Keyword injection:** every post published Jul 20 – Aug 2 carries "shark week" in caption line 1 (search traffic spikes for ~2 weeks around the event).
- **Copyright rule:** never re-upload Shark Week footage. On TikTok use native stitch/duet only; on IG react with own visuals + text.
- **Crisis rule still applies:** Shark Week coverage sometimes includes real incidents. Check `crisis.md` before each post that week.
- R4.2 (certificate reveal) lands inside this window — good. If the schedule slips, slip it INTO the window, not past it.

## 2. Comment Strategy (daily, 10 minutes)

At zero followers, witty comments under big accounts are the cheapest reach available. A deadpan Bureau comment under a viral shark video earns profile visits for free.

Routine: 10 min/day, comment on 3-5 FRESH posts (under 2-3 hours old — early comments surface higher) from a fixed watchlist.

Watchlist to build (10-15 accounts): major aquariums (e.g. Monterey Bay Aquarium — the tone-setter for this genre), NOAA/ocean agencies, ocean photographers and divers, shark-positive creators, big animal-meme pages.

Comment rules:

- 1-2 sentences, in-character, dry. Use `voice.md` patterns. Examples: `The Bureau has reviewed this footage. The shark was commuting.` / `Filed under: routine patrol, no objections received.` / `The Bureau notes the subject is having a quiet day, statistically.`
- Never under incident/injury news (crisis rule). Never argue. Never correct a creator publicly in a way that punches down.
- No links, no "check our page" — the profile visit comes from the joke or not at all.
- If a comment takes off, reply to responses in character; that thread is doing your marketing.

## 3. Creator Seeding via Personalized Wanted Posters

The outreach tool is already built: the Wanted generator. A personalized poster IS the pitch — funny, free, zero ask.

Target profile: micro-creators 5k-50k followers in ocean/diving/aquarium/animal-humor niches; actively posting; comment sections with real engagement. Avoid mega-accounts (ignored) and fear-content accounts (off-brand).

Cadence: 10 DMs/week, tracked in a simple list (name, platform, date, response). Expect a 10-20% response rate; 1-2 warm responses/week is success.

DM template (personalize the charge to their actual content — this is the whole trick):

> Official notice from the Shark Human Alliance: a case has been opened regarding [name]. Charge: [something specific and affectionate from their content, e.g. "petting a nurse shark without filing Form FIN-1"]. The poster is attached. No action is required, although the Bureau notes that framing it is customary.
>
> (Context from the humans behind the Bureau: we're a tiny fictional agency fixing shark PR, with real conservation behind the joke. The poster generator is free — sharkhumanalliance.com/wanted — in case your followers want to open cases of their own.)

Rules: never ask them to post; never mention the certificate; one follow-up maximum (a week later, in character: `The case remains open. The Bureau is patient.`); if they post organically, thank them in character and add them to the watchlist (section 2).

## 4. First-48-Hours Protocol (every Reel)

Early velocity decides distribution. For each Reel:

1. Publish when you can stay online for the next hour.
2. Post the card's PINNED COMMENT immediately.
3. Ask 3-5 friends to comment a verdict word (GUILTY / MISUNDERSTOOD / FILED / ACQUITTED) within the first hours — comments, not likes. Rotate who you ask.
4. Reply to every comment in the first 24h, in character (replies double the comment count).
5. Share the Reel to one relevant group chat / your own Story.

Do not: like-exchange pods, follow/unfollow, buying followers or engagement — all of it poisons the account's distribution profile permanently.

## 5. Paid Boost Rules

Purpose: prime the algorithm by amplifying a PROVEN post, never to rescue a flopped one.

- Trigger: a Reel beats the account's average completion rate AND draws organic comments from strangers.
- Budget: $50-100 per boost, IG Boost + TikTok Promote split, objective = profile visits / engagement (never "followers" campaigns).
- Frequency: max 1-2 boosts/month in the first quarter. Track `from=social_*` events on the site during boost windows to see if paid reach converts to `wanted_poster_generate`.
- Never boost: fact posts with third-party footage, anything during a crisis pause, anything with a time-limited joke.

## 6. Earned Media & Directories

One writeup can beat months of posting. Pitch AFTER there's minimal social proof (one mini-viral post, or first public registry entries).

Targets, in order:

1. **Novelty/humor blogs:** Bored Panda, Sad and Useless, similar "internet oddities" sites — they actively source quirky projects.
2. **Curation newsletters:** recommendation-style newsletters (Recomendo-type, web-curiosities digests).
3. **Free-tool directories & communities:** the Wanted generator qualifies as a free internet toy (e.g. r/InternetIsBeautiful — read each community's self-promo rules first and respect them; a banned domain is permanent).
4. **Gift guides (Q4):** the certificate is a natural "weird gifts" listicle item — pitch in October, not December.

Pitch template (short, sent as-is with 2-3 images):

> Subject: A fictional bureaucracy is issuing shark-protection paperwork (real conservation behind it)
>
> Hi [name] — we run the Shark Human Alliance: a deadpan fictional agency run by two plush sharks that issues official-ish "not a snack" certificates and free Wanted posters for humans who slander sharks. The paperwork is fictional; the shark conservation behind it is not. The free poster generator tends to be the thing people share: sharkhumanalliance.com/wanted. Happy to send more posters, images, or an interview with the Bureau's press spokesperson (he has prepared a statement).

Note: the "real conservation" claim in any press pitch must satisfy `strategy.md` Decision Filter #6 — until a named recipient org + committed % exists, phrase it as a forward commitment. Locking in that partner is the single biggest unlock for this whole section.

## What NOT To Do

- No bought followers, pods, or follow/unfollow — permanent damage for vanity numbers.
- No product (certificate) in any outreach — free tool only.
- No fear-hooks anywhere, including DMs and pitches (`voice.md` forbidden list applies off-platform too).
- No humor outreach during an active incident news cycle (`crisis.md`).
- No judging formats before 3-4 weeks of consistent posting.
