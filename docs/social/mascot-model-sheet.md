# Mascot Consistency Brief

Last updated: 2026-05-20

Use existing approved mascot visuals as the primary reference. This file is only a guardrail for new social scenes, AI generations, and composites.

Primary references:

- `public/mascots/homepage-hero-plush.png`
- `public/mascots/case-closed-share.png`

## Non-Negotiables

- Keep the same soft plush/chibi look as the website.
- Keep Finnley blue and Luna teal.
- Keep Luna's flower, coral scarf, and badge when framing allows.
- Keep both characters friendly and non-threatening.
- Add Bureau-world props: files, stamps, folders, forms, podium, redaction pen.

Avoid:

- realistic shark anatomy
- scary/aggressive poses
- visible sharp teeth
- horror lighting
- hyperrealistic 3D style
- inconsistent eye shape or body color
- switching to flat vector unless the whole template uses that style

## Finnley Mako

Press spokesperson. Optimist. Slightly offended by Hollywood.

Use for statements, field reports, press conference posts, case closure announcements, and optimistic certificate moments.

Do not give Finnley Luna's flower/scarf. Keep him blue, slightly larger, friendly, and public-facing.

## Luna Reef

Misunderstanding Prevention. Reads, initials, occasionally redacts.

Use for redactions, review notes, compliance jokes, quiet approvals, and controlled disapproval.

Do not make Luna frantic. Keep her teal, slightly smaller, organized, and calmly unimpressed.

## AI Generation Rules

- Use approved images as character references.
- Prefer editing from reference images over pure text prompting.
- Regenerate/rework if color, accessories, eye shape, or body proportions drift.
- Do not publish mascot-heavy posts without checking against the references.

Future asset need: create a small turnaround/reference set from the approved visuals before producing many mascot-heavy scenes. Minimum useful set is front, three-quarter, and side/profile for each mascot, plus calm/reviewing/statement expressions.

## Base Prompt Add-On

Use this after the specific scene:

`soft plush chibi shark ambassador style, consistent with existing Shark Human Alliance mascots, rounded friendly body, large expressive eyes with white highlights, gentle fabric texture, official-ish Bureau paperwork props, navy ink and cream paper palette, warm dry humor, no scary teeth, no aggression, no horror, no photorealistic shark anatomy`

## Negative Prompt

`scary shark, aggressive teeth, blood, horror, realistic predator, different character, wrong colors, missing Luna flower, missing Luna scarf, harsh neon, generic ocean mascot, plastic 3D toy, distorted eyes, unreadable extra text`

## Social Usage Rule

Not every post needs mascots. During launch, aim for about one mascot-heavy feed post per week. The Bureau world should still be recognizable even when Finnley and Luna are absent.
