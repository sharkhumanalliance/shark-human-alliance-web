# Social Voice Guide

Last updated: 2026-05-17

## Voice Formula

Dry bureaucracy + real conservation + soft absurdity.

The Bureau speaks as if shark-human relations are an ordinary administrative matter that has become mildly inconvenient.

## Brand Voice Rules

Sound official but not corporate. Be dry, not zany. Let the absurdity sit without explaining it. Keep facts precise and source-backed. Keep product mentions administrative.

## Sentence Shape

Good:

- "The Bureau has reviewed the situation."
- "No timely objection has been received."
- "This has been filed for the public record."
- "Luna has initialed the document without further comment."

Avoid:

- "We are so excited to announce..."
- "Tag your bestie if they need this!"
- "You won't believe this shark fact!"
- "Let's save sharks together!"

## Caption Rules

- No emoji in feed captions.
- No exclamation marks unless Finnley is directly quoted, and even then rarely.
- No explaining the joke after the joke.
- No guilt-heavy activism language.
- No clickbait.
- No brand voice that sounds like a normal DTC gift company.
- First line must work on its own.
- If the post is factual, the caption should not overstate the graphic.

## Forbidden Phrases

Do not use:

- "Hey guys"
- "You won't believe"
- "Tag a friend who"
- "Let's make a change"
- "Join the movement"
- "Limited-time offer"
- "Buy now"
- "This is hilarious"
- "We made this funny thing"
- "Ocean vibes"
- "Shark attack season"

Also avoid fear-based hooks:

- "Terrified of sharks?"
- "Don't get eaten"
- "Avoid shark attacks"

Never imply the product provides real safety.

## Approved Voice Patterns

### Bureau Memo

`Memo {number} - {officially absurd statement}.`

Example:

`Memo 0001 - The Bureau is operational. Filing has commenced.`

### Public Record

`Filed for the public record: {fact}.`

Example:

`Filed for the public record: sharks have had better centuries.`

### Luna

`Luna reviewed this. {dry outcome}.`

Examples:

- `Luna reviewed this. Six lines did not survive.`
- `Luna has initialed the document without visible enthusiasm.`
- `Forwarded to Luna. The Bureau regrets the delay.`

### Finnley

`Finnley has prepared a statement. {small caveat}.`

Examples:

- `Finnley has prepared a statement. He insists it is brief.`
- `Finnley remains optimistic. This is not legally binding.`
- `Finnley has reviewed the footage and blames cinema.`

### Wanted

`A case has been opened regarding {person/status}.`

Examples:

- `A case has been opened regarding an unprotected human.`
- `Subject appears confident. Documentation remains absent.`
- `The Bureau recommends immediate filing.`

### Product Bridge

`Close the case with paperwork.`

Examples:

- `If the accusation stands, paperwork may now be filed.`
- `The case may be resolved through Form FIN-1.`
- `Protection status can be improved administratively.`
