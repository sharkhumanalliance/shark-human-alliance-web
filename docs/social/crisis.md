# Crisis And Sensitivity Protocol

Last updated: 2026-05-20

This project jokes about fictional shark-human paperwork. It must never joke about real victims, real injuries, or real communities affected by a shark incident.

## Pause Rules

If a fatal or serious shark incident is in the news cycle:

1. Pause all scheduled humor, Wanted, and product-bridge posts for 48-72 hours.
2. Review scheduled Stories, Reels, and auto-posting queues immediately.
3. Continue only neutral conservation/fact posts if they are not tied to the incident, location, or victim.
4. Do not reference the incident for engagement.
5. Do not use "not food", "snack", "resume", "case closed", "protection granted", or similar jokes during the pause.

If unsure whether the incident qualifies as serious, pause.

## Response Rules

Never respond in-character to genuine distress, criticism about victims, or community grief.

Use plain human tone:

> We never mock victims or real shark incidents. Shark Human Alliance is a fictional paperwork project that funds shark conservation, but real harm is not part of the joke. We have paused scheduled humor and are reviewing the queue.

Then disengage unless a factual correction is needed.

## If Accused Of Trivializing Attacks

Do:

- acknowledge once
- state the boundary plainly
- mention conservation support without turning it into a sales pitch
- pause the relevant post if timing was poor

Do not:

- argue in-character
- make a joke
- debate grief
- defend the post repeatedly
- ask the critic to understand the brand voice

## If A Victim's Family Or Local Community Contacts The Account

1. Stop public replies.
2. Remove or pause any nearby humor that could reasonably feel connected.
3. Reply privately and plainly if appropriate.
4. Do not ask for details, proof, or emotional labor.
5. Keep the response short and human.

Suggested private reply:

> I'm sorry. We never want this project to add pain around a real incident. We have paused the relevant content and will review the queue before posting again.

## After The Pause

Restart with neutral content first:

- conservation fact
- impact transparency
- non-incident education

Wait before returning to "snack", "not food", Wanted, or fake protection jokes.
