## 1. Visual concept — austere archival entry

Same paper world as the rest (cream, navy ink, thin frame) so it still reads as a Bureau filing — but **stripped of comedic furniture**. The signal to the viewer is the *absence* of the usual stamp and quip.

Differentiation from the 2.2 stat cards (which also use big numbers): 2.2 was a busy multi-card set with deadpan notes and a `FILED` stamp; 3.2 is a **single, quiet, stamp-less card** with much more negative space and a more reverent typographic treatment. Where 2.2 says "the Bureau is amused," 3.2 says "the Bureau is, for once, just looking."

- One large quiet figure: `450 MILLION YEARS`.
- One plain statement beneath it.
- A small source line.
- **No stamp. No Bureau punchline. No mascots.**
- Generous margins; let the number breathe. Deep navy on cream; serif (Cormorant-Garamond-like) for reverence rather than the mono "form" look.
- Optional single restrained motif: a faint, simple line silhouette of a shark or a single fossil tooth, low-contrast, not decorative clutter.

Format exports: 4:5 feed (primary), 1:1 optional. A 9:16 Story works (quiet "on this day, the record shows…" Story).
