## 1. Visual concept — retro public-service poster

A vintage **PUBLIC NOTICE / PSA** poster in the mid-century civic-announcement style (think old transit or wartime public-information posters), restrained palette, **no mascots**. Distinct from the cream "form/notice" cards: this is a *poster*, so it can carry a bolder headline lockup and a little more design personality while staying period-correct and not loud.

- Headline: the PSA statement, large.
- A small `PUBLIC NOTICE` or `A MESSAGE FROM THE BUREAU` tab.
- Restrained colors (navy + cream + one muted accent — the Filing orange `#EE8A45` used sparingly).
- Optional period motif: a simple icon (a stylized résumé/document, or a calm shark silhouette that is friendly, not toothy).
- One stamp at most (`FILED` or none — a PSA can stand without a stamp).

Format exports: 4:5 feed (primary), 1:1 optional, 9:16 Story.
