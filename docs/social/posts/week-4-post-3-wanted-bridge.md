## 1. The mechanic — read this first (it determines whether the post converts)

**Instagram feed posts cannot carry clickable links.** So the CTA has to be routed deliberately:

- **Instagram:** set the **link in bio** to the Wanted page for this week, and say "link in bio" in the caption. Optionally also post a **Story with a link sticker** pointing to the same URL (Stories can link).
- **Facebook:** the feed post **can** include the clickable link directly in the caption.

**Canonical CTA URL:** `https://sharkhumanalliance.com/wanted?from=social_wanted`

- `from=social_wanted` is the canonical attribution source the site expects for social→Wanted traffic (per the calendar production checklist and `lib/analytics-events.ts` conventions).
- ⚠️ **Confirm before publishing (do not assume):** that the `/wanted` route reads and persists an inbound `from=` param for attribution. `CLAUDE.md` documents that `/purchase` reads/persists `from` on mount; the `/wanted` page's handling of an inbound `from=` is not explicitly documented. If `/wanted` does not forward it, either (a) add that handling, or (b) point the bio link at a URL that does (e.g. a `/purchase?...&from=social_wanted` entry is documented to work, but that skips the free Wanted step). **Recommended:** verify `/wanted?from=social_wanted` persists to `sessionStorage["sha_attribution_source"]`; if not, fix the route before this post goes live, because this is the first real attribution test.

Vintage, Wanted-poster world (this is the brand's signature viral template). Two viable directions:

- **A — "Now accepting filings" notice (recommended for the bridge).** A Bureau notice announcing the reporting channel is open, with a blank/!-marked Wanted poster pinned beside it: `WANTED — UNPROTECTED HUMAN`, silhouette, `CASE OPEN`. - **B — case-file desk scene.** A desk with a partly-filled Wanted poster, stamps, a `CASE OPENED` stamp. Warmer, busier; good if you want the "office at work" feel.

Shared: Wanted red (`#B94135`), deep poster brown (`#3A2515`) outer, cream poster paper (`#F6ECD8`), case number, one stamp (`CASE OPENED`). **No mascots required** (the poster is the star); a small Finnley "field officer" cameo is optional if you want warmth, but keep the poster dominant.

Format exports: 4:5 feed (primary), 9:16 Story (with link sticker — important for the IG CTA), 1:1 optional.

## 7. Engagement guidance (comments) — this is where the mechanic spreads

The Wanted/case mechanic spreads through verdict-style, in-world comment CTAs and tagging (`voice.md`). Lean in here, stay light.

- Encourage filing/tagging: reply to "tagging my friend" with `A case has been opened regarding the named human. Documentation remains absent.`
- Verdict bait (in the caption or pinned comment): `Guilty of being unprotected, or merely misunderstood? The Bureau will accept comments.`
- Accuse-back (reciprocity): if someone is "accused" in comments, invite them to file back — `The accused may file a counter-case. The Bureau is impartial and easily persuaded.`
- "Is this real / do I pay?" → clarify warmly: the Wanted poster is **free**; the certificate is a separate, optional bit of paperwork; proceeds will fund real shark conservation. Never imply the product provides real safety.
- Someone uncomfortable being named → de-escalate immediately, plain tone, drop the bit; offer to "close the case." Never push.
- Real shark-incident or distress → `crisis.md`, no humor.
