Background is clean modern paper-white (`#F8FAFC`) with one small vintage cue (Bureau header mark + the stamp), so it reads modern-hybrid, not full-vintage.

## 4. Pull-quote — options + recommendation

Luna's register is **drier and more deadpan than Finnley** — less emotion, more review-desk fatalism. All in her voice; the line is her first words on the record (parity with Finnley's "1975 film…").

**Deadpan options:**

1. `I read everything. I initial some of it. I redact the rest.` ★ **Recommended**
2. `The enthusiasm has been noted and removed.`
3. `Finnley issues the statements. I make sure they are survivable.`
4. `Nothing leaves this office unreviewed. Including this.`
5. `I prevent misunderstandings. It is a high-volume position.`
6. `Most misunderstandings are preventable. The Bureau has chosen to try anyway.`

**Warmer / cute variant (parity with Finnley, humor-first):**

7. `Finnley means well. Someone has to read it.`

**Recommendation:** Option **1** — `I read everything. I initial some of it. I redact the rest.`
It is the most character-defining, has clean rule-of-three deadpan rhythm, and *expands* the working caption tagline ("Reads, initials, occasionally redacts.") so image and caption reinforce each other. The third clause ("I redact the rest") is the natural target for the redaction-bar device in §3B.
Strong runners-up: **2** (purest deadpan, callback to reusable line #9) and **3** (defines the Finnley/Luna dynamic). Use **7** only if you want the launch beat warmer for parity.

**Attribution line (mirror of Finnley's "— FINNLEY MAKO, ON THE PUBLIC RECORD"):**
- Primary: `— LUNA REEF, MISUNDERSTANDING PREVENTION`
- Witty alt (vintage gag): `— LUNA REEF, THIS LINE LEFT UNREDACTED`

## 7. Engagement guidance (comments)

Luna replies **unlike Finnley**: dry, short, no emotion, no exclamation marks, no emoji. She acknowledges, she does not gush. One sentence max; often just a stamp-word.

Reusable Luna replies (playful comments only):
- `Noted.`
- `Reviewed. No changes.`
- `Filed.`
- `The comment has been initialed.`
- `Forwarded to the appropriate drawer.`
- `Objection received. Objection redacted.`
- (to a compliment) `The enthusiasm has been noted and removed.`

Do **not** use Bureau-deadpan for genuine criticism, distress, conservation questions, refunds, or real shark incidents — switch to plain human tone per `voice.md` and `crisis.md`. Never argue, never punch down.
