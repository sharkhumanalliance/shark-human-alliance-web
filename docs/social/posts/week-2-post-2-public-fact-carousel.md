## 1. Visual concept — vintage "public notice" carousel

A 5-card set that reads like a stack of official Bureau notices the public is invited to file away. **No mascots** — the joke lives in the paperwork (Bureau-driven mode). One consistent template across all slides so the swipe feels like one document series.

The swipe is built as a small argument, not a list: the Bureau presents the scale of human-caused harm (100M), raises the stakes (1 in 3 species), then turns — "and in return" — to fewer than 10. The final card files the complaint. Humor is carried by one dry Bureau annotation on each card; the two-word `For comparison.` on the `<10` card is the turn.

Shared system (identical on every slide):
- Aged cream paper (`#EFE7D4` / `#F6ECD8`), subtle grain and faint ink imperfections — tactile, not plastic.
- Navy ink (`#162D50`) text, thin Bureau frame, small `SHARK HUMAN ALLIANCE` header mark.
- One big figure per fact slide; one careful statement; one small **italic Bureau note**; one small source line.
- Swipe affordance: a faint `MORE ON FILE →` corner cue on slides 1–4.
- One stamp total across the set: `FILED` on slide 5 (the close), as the payoff.

Format exports: 4:5 (carousel standard). 1:1 set optional. No 9:16 (carousels are feed-only).

## 2. Slide-by-slide copy (exact strings — do not paraphrase)

Wording is locked to `sources.md`. **Bold** = large display figure; then the statement line; *italic* = the Bureau note; `SOURCE` = the mandatory in-graphic source line.

- Sub: `Filed by the Shark Human Alliance. Swipe to review the evidence.`

**Slide 2 — Human-caused mortality (Fact 1)**

**Slide 3 — Extinction risk (Fact 2)**

- Statement: `More than one-third of shark, ray, and chimaera species are threatened with extinction.`
- Bureau note (italic): `The Department regrets the size of the fraction.`  ← sources.md, Fact 2 Bureau wording
- Source: `SOURCE — DULVY ET AL., CURRENT BIOLOGY, 2021`
- (Use "shark, ray, and chimaera species" — not "one-third of all shark species".)

**Slide 4 — Human fatalities (Fact 3) — the turn**
- Display figure: `FEWER THAN 10`
- Statement: `Fewer than 10 human fatalities from unprovoked shark bites occur in most years.`
- Bureau note (italic): `For comparison.`  ← the turn; recontextualizes slides 2–3
- Source: `SOURCE — FLORIDA MUSEUM ISAF`
- (Always say "unprovoked shark bites".)

**Slide 5 — Bureau close (the filing)**
- Line: `The Bureau finds the ratio administratively embarrassing.`  ← reusable line #16
- Sub: `Save this. File it wherever you keep things the public should know.`
- Stamp: `FILED` (red or navy, angle −8°). Only stamp in the set.
- No figure.

Why this order: 2 and 3 build the scale of human-caused harm; 4 flips expectation (the public fears sharks; the figure is single digits); `For comparison.` makes the reader do the math themselves; 5 lands the Bureau's deadpan verdict. The contrast is the joke — the cards stage it instead of stating it.

## 4. The "turn" line — options + recommendation

The carousel's payoff is the dry annotation on slide 4 (`<10`) that recontextualizes everything before it. This is the load-bearing joke; choose carefully.

1. `For comparison.` ★ **Recommended** — two words, lets the reader do the math; maximal deadpan, minimal explaining.
2. `The Bureau notes that the public relations imbalance remains severe.` — sources.md Fact 3 Bureau wording; funnier but longer, competes with the figure.
3. `This is the number people are afraid of.` — names the fear directly; slightly more editorial.
4. (no note on slide 4; let slide 5 carry it) — cleanest, but wastes the turn.

**Recommendation: 1.** `For comparison.` is the whole post in two words — it forces the juxtaposition without explaining the joke (a voice.md rule). Use **2** only if you'd rather the card itself carry the verdict and soften slide 5.

Closing line (slide 5): `The Bureau finds the ratio administratively embarrassing.` is canon for exactly this ratio (reusable #16). Alternative if you want the close to cover all four cards: `Filed for the public record. Regrettably, the numbers are real.`

## 7. Engagement guidance (comments)

Bureau-voice post, so replies are dry and factual — but **never** deadpan over genuine conservation questions or real-incident comments (switch to plain human tone per `voice.md` / `crisis.md`).

- Genuine fact/conservation question → answer plainly and helpfully, cite `sources.md`, drop the bit.
- Playful "filed!" / "saved" comment → short Bureau reply: `Filed.` / `Added to the record.` / `The Bureau thanks you for your attention to this damp administrative issue.`
- "Source?" challenge → point to the on-card source line and the full citation in `sources.md`; stay factual, don't joke a sourcing question away.
- "But sharks ARE dangerous" pushback → stay factual and non-argumentative: restate the ISAF figure, no mockery. Per voice.md, never punch down.
- Real shark-incident or distress in comments → `crisis.md`, no humor.
