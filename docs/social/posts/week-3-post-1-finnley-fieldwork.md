## 4. Pull-quote — options + recommendation

Finnley's register is warm and optimistic (the contrast to Luna's deadpan). The line should announce fieldwork, land an actual turn (not just be sweet), and lead naturally into Saturday's filed report. It should frame the work as **protection, not surveillance** — see the tone note in §1.

1. `The Bureau has entered the field. Humans appear unaware they are being protected.` ★ **Recommended**
2. `Day one of field observations. No human has eaten anyone. Morale is high.` *(absurd inversion — flips the snack frame)*
3. `I have been authorized to conduct fieldwork. Luna has authorized very little else.` *(Finnley/Luna two-hander, dry-warm)*
4. `The Bureau has entered the field. So far, everyone is doing their best.` *(softest; charming but no turn)*

**Recommendation: 1.** It lands a real turn — the one-sided care of a protector the protected don't know they have — while staying unmistakably the optimist. It keeps the frame *protective* rather than surveillance (killing the "watching people at the beach" risk), gently echoes the product premise (protection) without selling, and sets up 3.3 directly: Saturday's report ("Subject: one human… confidence high") now reads as Finnley quietly taking notes for protective reasons. Use **3** to foreground the Finnley/Luna dynamic this week, or **2** for the punchiest standalone laugh.

**Attribution:** `— FINNLEY MAKO, FIELD OFFICER (SELF-APPOINTED)` (the "self-appointed" is the joke — nobody assigned him). Parity alternative: `— FINNLEY MAKO, ON THE PUBLIC RECORD` (matches his 1.3 attribution).
