# Brand And Social Kit

Last updated: 2026-05-31

## Core Principle

> Cute and funny first, mascots front and center, Bureau as the world, product last.

The social identity should look like an adorable fictional government office run by two expressive plush sharks. Lead with character and comedy; the official paperwork world is the comedic frame that makes the cuteness land, and what separates it from a generic shark account.

## Visual Tone

Use expressive, front-and-center plush mascots inside an official paperwork world: certificates, case files, stamps, cream paper, navy ink, redactions, mini desks and props. Finnley and Luna carry the emotion and the comedy; the paperwork gives it the deadpan-official frame.

Keep it cute and warm. Avoid: aggressive or scary shark visuals, gore, fearmongering, meme-template fonts, loud gradients, stock beach imagery, and overusing the SHA logo. Cartoon expressiveness is welcome; chaos that loses the official frame is not.

## Typography

The website already loads these fonts. Use them in social templates where possible.

| Font | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Cinzel | Certificate-style titles, "PUBLIC NOTICE", "MEMORANDUM", official headers |
| Cormorant Garamond | Formal copy, quotes, certificate language |
| Geist Sans | Clean readable captions inside graphics |
| Geist Mono | Case IDs, source lines, timestamps, form fields |
| Dancing Script | Signatures only; use sparingly |

Rules: uppercase mono for Bureau labels, serif for document authority, sans for readability, novelty fonts only for stamped text.

## Stamp Set

Create transparent PNG stamps. Navy = ordinary processing, red = urgent/rejected/Wanted, gold = certificate/ceremony.

Required MVP stamps:

1. `FILED`
2. `REVIEWED`
3. `REDACTED`
4. `PENDING`
5. `NO OBJECTIONS RECEIVED`
6. `CASE OPENED`
7. `CASE CLOSED`
8. `PROTECTION GRANTED`
9. `OBJECTION DENIED`
10. `FORWARD TO LUNA`
11. `RETURNED - RECIPIENT UNREACHABLE`

Stamp rules: usually one stamp per post; two only when the joke needs escalation. Keep stamp angle between -12 and +12 degrees.

## Template Set

### 1. Bureau Memo

Use for short announcements.

Elements: cream paper, `SHARK HUMAN ALLIANCE`, memo number, one dry paragraph, one stamp.

### 2. Public Notice Stat Card

Use for factual shark posts.

Elements: one large number, one careful fact statement, tiny source line from `sources.md`. Use mascots only if the fact needs softening.

### 3. Wanted Case

Use for virality and user-tagging.

Elements: Wanted red, case number, suspect silhouette or generated poster visual, clear CTA to open a case.

### 4. Finnley Statement

Use for optimistic public-facing quotes.

Elements: Finnley portrait, one quote, attribution line, `REVIEWED` or `FILED` stamp.

### 5. Luna Redacted

Use for dry redaction jokes.

Elements: official document, redacted blocks, only 1-3 visible lines, `REDACTED` or `FORWARD TO LUNA` stamp.

### 6. Field Report

Use for observational humor.

Elements: timestamp, subject status, documentation status, recommendation, signed `F.M.`

### 7. Conservation Receipt

Use for trust and transparency.

Elements: exact allocation number, plain explanation, no inflated claims, source or reporting note.

Gate: do not publish a Conservation Receipt (or any "the conservation is real" claim) until there is a real, citable allocation figure or a named recipient org with a committed %. Until then the claim must be a forward commitment, not a present-tense fact.

### 8. Case Closed / Product Bridge

Use once Wanted enters the funnel.

Elements: "case closed" status, certificate mention, product as paperwork resolution, no hard discount language.

### 9. Press Conference

Use for launches, milestones, public allocation updates, product openings.

Elements: Finnley at podium/microphone, one quote, `OFFICIAL STATEMENT`, optional Luna note.

## Asset MVP

Before launching the first feed post, prepare:

- 10 stamps as transparent PNGs.
- 3 paper textures: clean cream, stained cream, official ledger/grid.
- 9 social templates in Canva or Figma.
- Finnley transparent portrait.
- Luna transparent portrait.
- Finnley + Luna duo transparent or clean-background image.
- 4:5, 1:1, and 9:16 export variants.
- Source-line component for fact cards.
- Small SHA Bureau header component.

## Treatment Rules

Mascots must stay consistent with existing website assets. Do not make them scary, toothy, aggressive, hyperrealistic, or stylistically different between posts.

Documents should feel tactile, not plastic: slight grain, subtle ink imperfections, simple mobile-readable layouts.

Use small SHA markers, official labels, and stamps instead of large repeated logos.

Product appears as administrative resolution: file paperwork, close the case, issue certificate, improve shark-facing status. Avoid hard-sell language.
